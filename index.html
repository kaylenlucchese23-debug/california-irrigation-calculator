FAO-56 formula reference
ETo = Reference evapotranspiration (Penman-Monteith, in/month) Kc = Crop coefficient — dimensionless (FAO-56, Table 12) ETa = ETo × Kc (actual crop evapotranspiration) Blue water = max(0, ETa − Effective Precipitation) [irrigation demand] Green water = min(ETa, Effective Precipitation) [precip contribution] Acre-feet = Irrigation (in) × Acreage (acres) ÷ 12
CIMIS data source: cimis.water.ca.gov · Relevant stations for HUC8 18060005: Salinas (#2), Spreckels (#109), King City (#80). FAO Kc values: FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56.
